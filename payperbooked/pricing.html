THE MONTHLY CAP

Where the bill stops.

All trades reach the $499 cap at 16 confirmed bookings. $49 + $29 × 15 = $484, still under the cap. $49 + $29 × 16 = $513, so that month is $499.

WHAT COUNTS

What counts as a confirmed booking

A confirmed booking is a new customer's appointment booked onto the owner's calendar.

These do not count and are not billed:

  • Spam or robocalls
  • Existing customers already on the books
  • Duplicates
  • Wrong numbers or out-of-area callers
  • Bookings the customer cancels within 24 hours

You have 48 hours to dispute a booking that was counted and should not have been. If it should not count, it comes off that month's bill.
