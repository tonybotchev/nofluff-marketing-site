HOW A BOOKING IS COUNTED

Only the appointment we book.

A confirmed booking is an appointment our system books onto your calendar. A call that does not become an appointment is not billed. You have 48 hours to dispute a wrong or duplicate booking.

All trades reach the $499 cap at 16 confirmed bookings. $49 + $29 × 15 = $484, still under the cap. $49 + $29 × 16 = $513, so that month is $499.
