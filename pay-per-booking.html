03 / WHAT COUNTS

Only the appointment we book.

You have 48 hours to dispute a booking.

What counts

A confirmed booking is an appointment our system books onto your calendar. Billing counts only those appointments.

What does not count

A call that does not become an appointment is not a confirmed booking.

If it is wrong or a duplicate

You have 48 hours to dispute it. If it should not count, it comes off that month's bill.

What counts as a confirmed booking?

No. Setup is $0 and there is no contract. You can cancel anytime, effective at the end of the current billing month.

You pay $49 plus $29 per confirmed booking until the month reaches $499. The $499 cap applies to every trade, roofing and water/fire restoration included.

Bookings past the cap are free for the rest of that month.
