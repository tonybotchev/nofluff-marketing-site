What counts as a confirmed booking?

An appointment our system books onto your calendar. Billing counts only those appointments. You have 48 hours to dispute a booking.

What if a booking is wrong or a duplicate?

You have 48 hours to dispute it. If it should not count, it comes off that month's bill.

Is there a setup fee or a contract?

No. Setup is $0 and there is no contract. You can cancel anytime, effective at the end of the current billing month.

How does the monthly cap work? Why is roofing or restoration $999?

You pay $49 plus $29 per confirmed booking until the month reaches $499. Roofing and water/fire restoration use a $999 cap. The booking price stays $29. Only the cap is higher.

What happens after I hit the cap?

Bookings past the cap are free for the rest of that month.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month.

Which trades do you serve? Do you do lawn care?

HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, garage door, water/fire restoration, and other home-service trades. Not for lawn or landscaping.

Which towns do you serve?

Celina, Prosper, McKinney, Frisco, Anna, Melissa, Little Elm, Aubrey and Denton.

Do you serve other areas?

Not yet. We're starting along the US-380 corridor and plan to add more North Texas areas. Call to get on the list.

How many spots are open?
