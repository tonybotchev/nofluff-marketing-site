What counts as a confirmed booking?

A confirmed booking is a new customer's appointment booked onto the owner's calendar. These do not count and are not billed: spam or robocalls, existing customers already on the books, duplicates, wrong numbers or out-of-area callers, and bookings the customer cancels within 24 hours.

What if a booking is wrong or a duplicate?

Duplicates are not billed. For anything else that was counted and should not have been, you have 48 hours to dispute it. If it should not count, it comes off that month's bill.

Is there a setup fee or a contract?

No. Setup is $0 and there is no contract. You can cancel anytime, effective at the end of the current billing month.

How does the monthly cap work?

You pay $49 plus $29 per confirmed booking until the month reaches $499. The $499 cap applies to every trade, roofing and water/fire restoration included.

What happens after I hit the cap?

Bookings past the cap are free for the rest of that month.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month.

Which trades do you serve?

HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, garage door, water and fire restoration, and other home-service trades. Every trade uses the same price. See pricing.

Do you offer a pay per booking answering service for HVAC in North DFW?

Yes. PayPerBooked answers calls and books appointments for HVAC companies in North DFW, in Celina, Prosper, McKinney, Frisco, Anna, Melissa, Little Elm, Aubrey and Denton. Plumbing, electrical, roofing, garage door, and water and fire restoration use the same price: $0 setup, $49 a month, $29 per confirmed booking, and a $499 monthly cap.

Which towns do you serve?

North DFW towns on the US-380 corridor: Celina, Prosper, McKinney, Frisco, Anna, Melissa, Little Elm, Aubrey and Denton. The list is on this page under towns.

Do you serve other areas?

Not yet. We're starting along the US-380 corridor and plan to add more North Texas areas. Call to get on the list.
